# MyPresso

A playful presentation maker for kids. Pick a topic, choose a look, add words, stickers, pictures and drawings, then press **Show time!** to present with transitions, dancing stickers, quizzes and confetti.

## Made for young children

A grown-up picks the child's year group and reading stage once. MyPresso then changes its slides, words and help to suit them.

| Year 1 stage | What the child gets |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Starting out | One short sentence per slide, filled in by tapping picture words. No typing needed. |
| 🌿 Getting going | Sentence starters with ____ gaps and a word helper for spelling. |
| 🌳 Writing on my own | Question prompts and a checklist for capital letters, full stops and joining words. |

Questions, words and slides can be read aloud. Other year groups are coming soon.

## Using it

Open the website, or download `index.html` and double-click it. There is nothing to install.

Shows are saved in the browser on the device being used. They are not uploaded anywhere, and clearing the browser's data deletes them.

## Development

Everything lives in one file, `index.html`, written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
